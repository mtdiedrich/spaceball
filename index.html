On 21 May 2025, Oklahoma City Thunder guard Shai Gilgeous-Alexander (colloquially, SGA) accepted the Michael Jordan trophy, awarded to the National Basketball Association's Most Valuable Player.

SGA received 913 of a possible 1000 points from MVP voters, fueled by his 32.7 points per game that propelled the Thunder to a 68-14 record and the number one seed in the Western Conference.

SGA would go on to lead the Thunder to a victory in the NBA Finals, becoming the 11th player to win both the regular season and Finals MVP awards in the same season.
